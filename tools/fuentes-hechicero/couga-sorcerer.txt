Overview
The Sorcerer is a ranged glass cannon that casts both fire and ice. Fire does the damage — Fire Mark marks the target and Blaze cashes it in — while ice slows, roots and freezes so nothing reaches you. Half of your damage bonuses depend on mana: above 50% MP you crit more, above 25% you deal 20% more damage.
The build in one sentence
Hold one macro that keeps Element Enhancement and Wish of Concentration running and alternates Flame Arrow with your fire and ice lines so MP stays above half; cast Hellfire by hand.
Play in target mode. With a locked target the basic attack Flame Arrow keeps firing by itself after every skill and restores MP. In action mode it stops after each skill and you have to click again.
Two stages. In the first days at 45 your skills sit at 8–10 with one specialization each — take the mana and speed picks named in the cards. The card levels and the two highlighted picks are the later target (16), from EUTOPIA's global setup and Korean guides.
The early setup and the macro follow aLuckyRO's Sorcerer beginner guide (video, September 15, Taiwan client) and his global update of October 2; the skill priority and the level-16 picks follow EUTOPIA's global build (level 45, item level 1,260, October 3), who tops the damage charts in his groups. Skill names and data are from the global client.
Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.
Global Season 1: what changes
Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Sorcerer:
4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Taiwan and Korea play six; the four kept here are the ones both sources level first.
Skills at 16, not 20. Arcana and rings are what push skills past 16, and they come slowly.
Mana is tight at the start. About 3,000 MP and no cooldown reduction: the early picks are about MP and Skill Speed, not damage.
PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

Passives

Why
Robe of Flame
Accuracy, PvE Damage Boost and Double Chance — the first passive to max.
Fire Mark
Applies Fire Mark, which Blaze needs, and burns the target. Max it.
Robe of Earth
Max MP and Critical Hit while MP is 50% or more. Max it.
Grace of Enhancement
+20% PvE Damage Boost while MP is 25% or more, and extra hits.
Vitality Evaporation
Extra damage while the target is above 70% HP — strong early, when fights are short.
Cold Snap
Extra damage on slowed targets — Ice Chain keeps them slowed.
Robe of Cold
Defense and a slow on attackers — raids.
Robe of Earth and Grace of Enhancement switch off when your mana runs low — never play at 10–20% MP.
Gear & stats
Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy.
Watch Max MP and MP regen until your rotation stops running dry.
Which gear tier to chase is still open until the global launch.
Daevanion boards
Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.
Priority: orange Combat Speed and Cooldown Reduction, then Attack and Damage Boost, then Critical Damage Boost; after that the blue nodes of Hellfire , Firestorm , Bittercold Wind , Blaze , Wish of Concentration and the green Robe of Earth .
Skip Multi-Hit and the defensive orange nodes — a Sorcerer wants flat damage; pick up Accuracy later.
Priority: blue nodes for Frost , Winter's Shackles , Firestorm .
No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.
Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it.

Macro & rotation
? The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.
AION 2 has two separate tools:
Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority . The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

2. In-game macro
Bind a macro key in Settings → Key settings → Gameplay → Macro. Every step with the same delay:
1 Buff line 4
2 Concentration line 5
3 Flame Arrow R
4 Fire line 1
5 Flame Arrow R
6 Ice line T
7 Flame Scattershot 3
Delay: 10 ms with a ping under 50; 40–50 ms with a ping of 80–100 and more.
How to check it: watch your character while the macro runs. Afterimages ("shadows") behind it mean animations are being cancelled and the delay is fine.
Why two Flame Arrows: they refill mana between the expensive skills, so MP stays above 50% and both mana passives stay on.
The steps come from aLuckyRO's macro window (September 15, Taiwan client); Frost and Frost Burst were taken out after EUTOPIA's global setup. Flame Scattershot as the last step follows what aLuckyRO says (it fires by itself on a staggered boss).
3. What you actually press
Hold The macro key — nothing else.
By hand Hellfire : stop, charge it fully, go back to the macro.
Low mana If MP still drops, let go of the macro, fire a few Flame Arrow s and continue.
4. Later: burst by hand
Once your gear carries cooldown reduction, Korean players take the buffs out of the macro and open a burst window by hand:
Delayed Explosion just before the tank pulls.
Element Enhancement and Wish of Concentration .
Fire Wall + Cold Storm under the boss.
Bittercold Wind , then Winter's Shackles for the +20% damage boost.
Hellfire charged to 3, then the macro.
Recap
First days: skills at 8–10 with the mana and speed picks; one macro with the buffs inside; target mode.
Later: Hellfire, Firestorm, Bittercold Wind, Blaze, Wish of Concentration, Winter's Shackles, Flame Arrow 16; Ice Chain 12 or off the bar.
Stigmas: Element Enhancement 20, Cold Storm 10, Fire Wall 10, Delayed Explosion 10.
Mana: keep it above 50% — that is damage, not comfort.
How to fight while leveling
Packs: Bittercold Wind (root + pull) → Hellfire → Ice Chain until they drop.
Elites: Fire Mark → Blaze → Firestorm , kiting with Ice Chain slows.
Mana: use target mode so the basic attack keeps running, and weave Flame Arrow.
Low HP: Steel Barrier and distance — the Sorcerer has no heal.
