Overview
The Assassin is a dagger burst class that lives on buffs. Hits engrave Insignias on the target, Insignia Explosion cashes them in, and Heart Gore fires on crits and resets itself on the next crit. The biggest damage bonus comes from hitting the target in the back, so positioning matters more than on any other class.
The build in one sentence
Stay behind the boss, weave Quick Slice all the time, press Insignia Explosion the moment it is ready and Heart Gore on every crit — and put everything you have into the 20 seconds of Illusive Clone, when Heart Gore has no cooldown at all.
What to expect in Season 1
Burst, then a gap. On KR/TW the level-25 tier of Illusive Clone lets good players keep it up permanently. Global stops at 20: about 20 seconds of burst on a 1 min 30 s cooldown, and between windows you play fillers while Heart Gore depends on crit luck.
The buffs don't line up. Swift Contract and Savage Fang come back in a minute, Illusive Clone in a minute and a half — you keep deciding whether to wait or to press.
Back uptime is damage. On a dummy it is 100%; in a real fight expect 60–80%, and every hit from the front loses the Rear Smite bonus.
Fragile. No block, little defense — mistakes that a Gladiator survives kill you.
Arthars' verdict: a fun, high-skill class that needs several times more work than a Gladiator or a Sorcerer for the same damage or less. Pick it because you like the playstyle, not for the numbers.
The PvE mode follows Arthars Gaming's Assassin video for the global launch (September 30, recorded on the Taiwan server), read frame by frame; the video has no captions, so its text was taken from the audio by speech recognition and every name and number checked against the screen.

Global Season 1: what changes
Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Assassin:
4 stigma slots, capped at 20. All four go to damage: Illusive Clone, Swift Contract, Savage Fang and Triniel's Dagger.
No permanent Illusive Clone. The tier that shortens its cooldown on back crits is level 25 — not in global.
Little cooldown reduction at the start. That is why Storm Rampage goes to 16 and Triniel's Dagger is used as a cooldown tool, not for its damage.
Longer fights. Bosses that die in 30 seconds on Taiwan will take minutes — you will see several burst windows and the gaps between them.
PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.
Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier.

Gear & stats
Rings: the only place for active skills — Heart Gore, Quick Slice, Insignia Explosion, Ambush. They take the key skills from 14 to 16.
Weapon and guard: never put skills there — their damage lines are worth more than one skill level.
Other pieces: Rear Smite first; any of the next three passives is fine. Don't chase a perfect set in Season 1 — global gear has one line less and also rolls Might, Precision and Combat Speed there.
Critical Hit matters more than on other classes: Heart Gore resets only on a crit.
Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy.

The Parchment is the grind. All three skills you want at 20 sit on this one card, and a Season 1 card lets you choose only two of its four lines. Leveling the card adds four random +1s — hope for an even spread (+2 on each) rather than +4 on one: the Chalice can then add the missing +2. If it doesn't work out, keep Heart Gore and Insignia Explosion at 20 and leave Quick Slice at 16.
The Compass and the Bell are relaxed: nothing on them has to reach 20. All these cards are replaced when Season 2 brings new ones.
Daevanion boards
Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.
Priority: blue nodes for Heart Gore, Insignia Explosion, Quick Slice, then Ambush and Storm Rampage; orange nodes next.

Macro & rotation?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.
AION 2 has two separate tools:
Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

3. What you actually press
HoldThe macro key, and spam Quick Slice yourself — every hit shortens Insignia Explosion.
BurstIllusive Clone + Swift Contract + Savage Fang together, then Triniel's Dagger straight away to cut their cooldowns. For the next 20 seconds it is Heart Gore after Heart Gore.
Between windowsSwift Contract and Savage Fang come back before Illusive Clone. Either hold them for the next Clone (bigger burst) or use them on cooldown (steadier damage) — there is no agreed answer; on short fights hold them.
StaggerStorm Rampage on a staggered boss, weaving Quick Slice — it pulls every cooldown down by seconds.
PositionShadowstrike, Flash Slice and Infiltrate all put you behind the target. Use them to stay there, not as damage buttons.
Where the damage comes from in Arthars' test on a dummy: Heart Gore about 30%, Insignia Explosion about 25%, everything else 6% or less each.
Recap
Skills: Heart Gore, Insignia Explosion, Quick Slice 16 → 20; Ambush and Storm Rampage 16; the rest 12.
Stigmas: Illusive Clone 20, Swift Contract 15 → 20, Savage Fang 15, Triniel's Dagger 10.
Passives on gear: Rear Smite, then Exploit Weakness, Assault Stance, Impact Hit.
Play: stay behind the boss, burst inside Illusive Clone, Triniel's Dagger right after the buffs.
How to fight while leveling
Packs: Savage Fang (5 Insignias) → Insignia Explosion → Whirlwind Slice; Throw Shadowblade resets on a kill for the next pull.
Elites: get behind → Ambush → Savage Roar to stack Insignias → Insignia Explosion → Heart Gore on crits.
Mana: Quick Slice restores MP — keep it on LMB.
Low HP: Shadow Fall absorbs HP; Defiance heals when you're stuck.
Leveling macro?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.
While leveling, before Heart Gore reaches 16, a simpler setup works from level 1: the Insignia line in the in-game macro on the middle mouse button, Quick Slice on LMB and Heart Gore on Q. For farming mobs use Throw Shadowblade (2) and RMB. Early build at 1–45: Quick Slice, Savage Roar, Insignia Explosion and Heart Gore to 12, everything else to 8.
